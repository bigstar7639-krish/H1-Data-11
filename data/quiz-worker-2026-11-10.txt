Q+A Katha - Pazhamozhi 🧠

Question: What was the first ever entry written for the SCP Foundation collaborative writing project?

Answer: SCP-173 - Entertainment: Books. Itha kelvi Pazhamozhi pathi - Answer SCP-173. Oru kaalathula oru oor la Pazhamozhi nu oru periya vishayam irunthuchu, yaarum atha purinjikama irunthanga, oru chinna paiyan thaan Pazhamozhi oda arumaiya purinjikittan, avan Pazhamozhi pathi ellaar kitta yum pesi puriya vachan, mella mella oor makkal ellam Pazhamozhi ah purinjikittanga, antha oor ye Pazhamozhi nala azhaga maariduchu, ithula irunthu namakku theriyura vishayam enna na, Pazhamozhi ah purinjikita life ye azhagahum.
Q+A Katha - Reading 🧠

Question: Which actress's real name was Frances Ethel Gumm?

Answer: Judy Garland - Celebrities. Itha kelvi Reading pathi - Answer Judy Garland. Reading pathi kelvi patta oru ponnu, ava daily Reading pathi yosichittu iruntha, oru naal ava oru puthu book padichu Reading oda unmaiya purinjikita, athula irunthu ava life maariduchu, ava bayapadama Reading ah face panna aarambicha, ava friends ellam ava maariya paathu aachariyapattanga, kadasiya ava Reading la jeyichu santhosham ah iruntha, ithu thaan namakku kathai sollura paadam.
Q+A Katha - Writing 🧠

Question: What city did the monster attack in the film, "Cloverfield"?

Answer: New York, New York - Entertainment: Film. Itha kelvi Writing pathi - Answer New York, New York. Oru naal oru chinna oor la oru paiyan irunthan, avan manasula Writing pathi neraya yosichittu irunthan, yaar kitta sollurathu nu theriyama thaniya irunthan, appo avanukku oru nalla friend kedaichan, antha friend avan kooda pesi avan manasa purinjikittan, kadasiya avan Writing la irunthu nalla purithaloda sirichuttu veetuku ponan, ithu thaan Writing oda azhagu, kashtam vanthalum nambikkaiya iruntha ellam sari aayidum nu avan purinjikittan.
