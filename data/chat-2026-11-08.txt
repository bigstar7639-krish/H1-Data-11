## 1️⃣ Physics‑la Power = Energy / Time  
**Formula:**  
\[
P = \frac{E}{t}
\]  

- **E** = Energy (joules, J)  
- **t** = Time (seconds, s)  
- **P** = Power (watts, W) – 1 W = 1 J/s  

**Example:** Oru light bulb 60 W ah irukku, adhu 60 J energy‑a 1 second‑la use panrathu.

| **Electrical Power** | Voltage × Current (P = V × I) | Watt (W) |
